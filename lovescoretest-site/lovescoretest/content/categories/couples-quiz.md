A good **couple quiz** does not grade your relationship. It starts a conversation you might otherwise keep postponing. Pick a quiz below, answer honestly, and use the result as a gentle way to talk about feelings, habits and hopes. Every quiz here is free, takes only a few minutes, and is made for fun, not professional advice.

## **Which Couple Quiz Should You Take?**

Start with the question that feels closest to home. If you keep wondering about your own feelings, the [do I still love him quiz](/do-i-still-love-him-quiz) helps you sort love from habit. If you are single and wondering whether you are ready for a new relationship, try the [soulmate readiness test](/soulmate-readiness-test) instead. Both quizzes give a clear result you can reflect on at your own pace.

## **Couple Quizzes and Couple Tests**

Quizzes explore feelings and readiness, while tests compare answers side by side. When you want a shared score, the [couple compatibility test](/couple-compatibility-test) lets both partners answer the same questions. Curious who shows love more often? The [who loves more test](/who-loves-more-test) looks at affection, effort and support. Couples apart can check their bond with the [long distance relationship quiz](/long-distance-relationship-quiz). You can browse every option on the [couple tests](/couple-tests) page.

## **How to Use Your Results**

Treat every result as a conversation starter, not a verdict. Talk about the answers that surprised you, laugh about the ones that feel too accurate, and retake a quiz later to see what changed. Honest answers give the most useful results, so choose what is true today, not what you wish were true.

## **Couple Quiz FAQs**

**Are these couple quizzes free?**
Yes. Every quiz on this page is free to take, with no sign-up needed.

**How long does a couple quiz take?**
Most quizzes take about three minutes. Answer at a relaxed pace and talk through the result together.

**Can a quiz tell us if our relationship will last?**
No quiz can predict that. These quizzes are entertainment and reflection tools, not professional advice. They can highlight topics worth discussing, but real answers come from honest conversation over time.
