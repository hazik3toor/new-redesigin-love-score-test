## What LoveScoreTest.com is

LoveScoreTest.com is a small collection of free, name- and sign-based compatibility calculators: the Love Score, the Friendship Score, and the Zodiac Compatibility test. Each one takes what you enter, runs it through a lighthearted algorithm right in your browser, and gives you a percentage and a playful message to share with friends.

## What it isn't

None of the calculators on this site are scientific, psychological, or astrological assessments. They don't measure real relationships, real friendships, or real astrology — they're a fun way to spark conversation, not a tool for making decisions about your relationships. Every tool page says this plainly in its own FAQ section too.

## How the tools work

Each calculator uses a deterministic, character- or selection-based formula that runs entirely client-side in JavaScript. Nothing you type or select is ever sent to a server, logged, or stored — refresh the page and it's gone, unless you choose to share your result yourself using the share buttons.

## How the site is funded

LoveScoreTest.com is free to use and supported by third-party advertising shown on each page. Ads don't affect your calculator results in any way.
