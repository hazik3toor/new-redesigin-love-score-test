## Overview

LoveScoreTest.com ("we," "our," "this site") provides free entertainment compatibility calculators: Love Score, Friendship Score, and Zodiac Compatibility. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the site.

## Information you enter into the calculators

The names or zodiac signs you type or select into any calculator are processed entirely in your browser using JavaScript. They are never transmitted to, received by, or stored on any server we operate. Closing or refreshing the page clears them completely.

## Cookies

This site uses a cookie-consent banner and a small amount of local browser storage to remember your cookie preference (accepted or declined). Beyond that preference, we do not set our own tracking cookies. Third-party advertising partners (see below) may set their own cookies or use similar technologies subject to their own policies.

## Advertising

LoveScoreTest.com displays advertising served by third-party ad networks, including Adsterra. These networks may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to serve ads and measure performance. We do not control, and are not responsible for, the specific data practices of third-party ad networks. You can review Adsterra's own privacy practices on their website, and you can typically manage ad personalization through your browser or device settings.

## Analytics

If analytics tools are added to this site in the future, this policy will be updated to describe what's collected and why, before any such tool goes live.

## Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Because the calculators require no sign-up, login, or personal data entry beyond a name or sign choice that is never transmitted, no personal information is collected from any user regardless of age.

## Your choices

- You can decline non-essential cookies using the cookie banner shown on the site.
- You can clear your browser's local storage at any time to reset your cookie preference.
- You can use browser extensions or settings to block third-party ad content if you prefer.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.
